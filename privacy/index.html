YOUR PRIVACY

This GitHub Pages preview does not collect visitor analytics and has no sign-in service. The policy below describes the full hosted application.

A clear view of
what we collect.

Videos are available without an account. If you allow analytics, we record page views, video play events, a random session identifier, device type, referring website hostname and country when available. We do not use this to identify you by name, collect precise location or store your IP address in the analytics database.

Session counts are approximate and are not a count of identifiable people. You can change your analytics choice from the footer. Declining does not affect video playback.

Previously submitted project inquiries are retained for BloomBrain Media to respond and are visible only in the authorized admin dashboard. This website no longer accepts project inquiries through a contact form. Videos are currently delivered from bloombrainmedia.com, whose hosting provider receives normal media requests.

The dashboard reports the last 30 days of analytics. Stored inquiry records remain available to the agency. Email bloombrainmedia@gmail.com for a data access or deletion request.
